# Backoffice Not Loading

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Backoffice |
| **Escalations (1 Sep to 16 Nov 2026)** | 23 |
| **Last updated** | 25 Nov 2026 |

## Symptom

Agents report one of three scenarios:

1. **Cannot log in.** The agent starts a shift and cannot log in to Backoffice. Other tools work fine.
2. **Sudden logout.** Backoffice worked, then the agent was logged out. The agent cannot log back in, or sees an error code.
3. **No access after migration.** Backoffice moved to a new platform and the agent has no access to it yet.

## Quick Checks for Agents

### Scenarios 1 and 2: cannot log in or sudden logout

**Step 1: Ask other agents**

Check whether other agents use Backoffice normally.

- If other agents also have the problem, Backoffice has an internal issue or ongoing maintenance. Wait and inform the IT team.
- If only you have the problem, continue with the steps below.

**Step 2: Check your VPN**

Confirm that your VPN works properly. Backoffice is strictly tied to the VPN connection.

**Step 3: Close the tab and open it again**

Close the Backoffice tab and reopen it. Confirm that you use the correct URL.

**Step 4: Update the browser and clear the cache and cookies**

1. Update your browser.
2. Clear the cache and cookies.
3. Try to log in again.

If the problem continues, escalate to the IT team.

### Scenario 3: no access to the new platform

**Step 1: Check the address and the VPN**

1. Confirm that you use the address of the new platform, not the old one.
2. Confirm that your VPN connects to the right server.

**Step 2: Escalate a permission error**

If you see "permission denied" or "access denied" when you log in to the new Backoffice, escalate to the IT team. IT grants the access.

## Escalate to IT When

- Other agents also cannot use Backoffice. Inform IT about the outage.
- You complete all four steps for scenarios 1 and 2, and the problem continues.
- You see "permission denied" or "access denied" on the new platform.

## Include in the Escalation

- The error code or message on your screen
- The time the problem started
- The steps you already tried

IT investigates each case further.
