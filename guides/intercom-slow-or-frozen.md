# Intercom Is Slow or Freezing

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Intercom |
| **Escalations (1 Sep to 16 Nov 2026)** | 64 |
| **Last updated** | 25 Nov 2026 |

## Symptom

Intercom runs very slowly. Agents report these signs:

- Switching from one chat to another takes much longer than normal.
- The text of a chat takes more than 10 seconds to load.

## Quick Checks for Agents

Complete the steps in order. Stop when the problem is gone.

### Step 1: Check the Intercom status page

Open the [Intercom Status Page](https://www.intercomstatus.com/) and look for an ongoing outage or service disruption. If Intercom reports an issue, wait for Intercom to resolve it.

### Step 2: Refresh the page and restart the browser

1. Refresh the page with `Ctrl + R` on Windows or `Cmd + R` on Mac.
2. If the issue continues, close and reopen the browser.
3. Try a private or incognito window.

### Step 3: Check your internet connection

1. Test your speed on [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net/).
2. Restart your router if the connection is unstable.
3. Switch to a different network, such as a mobile hotspot, and check again.

### Step 4: Clear the browser cache and cookies

In Chrome:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Privacy and security**, then **Clear browsing data**.
2. Select **Cookies and other site data** and **Cached images and files**.
3. Click **Clear data**.
4. Restart the browser and log in again.

### Step 5: Try a different browser or device

1. If you use Chrome, test in Firefox, Edge, or Safari.
2. If possible, log in from another computer to find out whether the issue is specific to your setup.

### Step 6: Disable browser extensions and VPN

Ad blockers, script blockers, VPNs, and similar extensions interfere with Intercom. Disable all extensions, then refresh the page.

### Step 7: Check system resource usage

High CPU or RAM usage slows the browser. Close unneeded apps and browser tabs.

- **Windows:** press `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to open Task Manager.
- **Mac:** open Activity Monitor.

### Step 8: Restart your computer

A restart clears software glitches and frees system resources.

## Escalate to IT When

The problem continues after you complete all eight steps.

## Include in the Escalation

- A screenshot of the AWS connection health
- A screenshot with the VPN page open

IT investigates each case further.
